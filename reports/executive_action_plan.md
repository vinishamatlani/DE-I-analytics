# Executive Action Plan

## Purpose

This document translates the synthetic analytics into measurable
areas for organisational follow-up.

The recommendations are illustrative and are not claims about any
real organisation.

---

# 1. Overall Inclusion

The synthetic workforce dataset produces an Inclusion Index of
68.2/100.

The five dimensions are:

| Dimension | Score |
|---|---:|
| Belonging | 75.1 |
| Inclusive Leadership | 74.8 |
| Employee Voice | 66.3 |
| Psychological Safety | 64.3 |
| Fairness & Opportunity | 60.6 |

## Interpretation

Fairness & Opportunity is the lowest-scoring dimension in this
synthetic dataset.

This makes equitable access to development and perceived fairness
a useful area for further investigation.

---

# 2. Workforce Gaps

The largest observed negative gaps in the synthetic dataset include:

| Segment | Gap vs Company |
|---|---:|
| 6–10 years tenure | -3.2 |
| Commercial | -3.1 |
| Non-binary / self-described | -2.4 |
| Operations | -2.4 |
| 3–5 years tenure | -2.1 |

These differences should be treated as signals for further
investigation rather than explanations of why the differences exist.

Segment-level results should be examined alongside sample size,
context and qualitative information.

---

# 3. Associated Factors

Psychological Safety has the strongest observed correlation with
the separate overall inclusion outcome in this synthetic dataset:

**r = 0.372**

Other observed correlations include:

| Dimension | Correlation |
|---|---:|
| Psychological Safety | 0.372 |
| Belonging | 0.356 |
| Inclusive Leadership | 0.353 |
| Employee Voice | 0.297 |
| Fairness & Opportunity | 0.272 |

These are statistical associations.

They should not be interpreted as proof that changing one dimension
will cause the overall inclusion outcome to change.

---

# 4. Proposed Measurement Areas

## Area A — Psychological Safety

### Objective

Create conditions where employees can raise concerns, challenge
existing practices and ask for support.

### Measures

- Psychological Safety score
- Psychological Safety favourable %
- Employee Voice score
- Segment-level gaps
- Change over time

---

## Area B — Employee Voice

### Objective

Increase opportunities for employees to contribute perspectives
and receive visible follow-up.

### Measures

- Employee Voice score
- Voice favourable %
- Survey participation
- Feedback-response rate
- Follow-up completion rate

---

## Area C — Fairness & Opportunity

### Objective

Improve transparency and perceived fairness around development
and career opportunities.

### Measures

- Fairness & Opportunity score
- Fairness favourable %
- Career-development participation
- Promotion fairness perception
- Segment-level opportunity gaps

---

# 5. DE&I Event Measurement

The synthetic event dataset contains eight events.

Current aggregate measures include:

- 63.3% attendance
- 0.70 average knowledge gain
- 4.21/5 average satisfaction
- 74.7% repeat participation

These metrics provide a starting point for evaluating participation,
learning and participant experience.

Future evaluation could connect event participation with later
survey responses where an appropriate longitudinal evaluation design
exists.

---

# 6. Recommended Measurement Cycle

Use the following cycle:

**Baseline**

↓

**Intervention**

↓

**Follow-up measurement**

↓

**Segment analysis**

↓

**Qualitative investigation**

↓

**Adjust intervention**

↓

**Repeat measurement**

The objective is to create a continuous measurement loop rather than
treating a single survey as a final conclusion.
